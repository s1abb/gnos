GNOS
GNOS (GreenNet OptiSim) is the only platform that contains Rail and Road physics engines integrated with a fully fledged agent-based modelling simulator in one tool.

This enables a full system digital twin to be developed for any operation with the ability to drill down to any level of detail to answer a host of industry problems.

Learn more at the GNOS homepage

Key Features & Capabilities
Discrete Event Simulation (DES)
A fully integrated DES allows for simulation of complex process interactions with all other GNOS systems.

Key features include:

Overall system capacity modelling and reporting
Capacity to define and track key inputs and outputs
Utilisation rates, capacity metrics and bottleneck analysis for distinct assets and at system level
Integration with Rail movements & Mobile Assets
Customisable Reporting and asset tracking
Agent / Discrete Simulation methodology
Flowchart modelling methodology with pre-defined blocks and parameters
Programmable Logic Blocks
Rail Simulation
GNOS offers a comprehensive rail fleeting and capacity modelling package.

Key features include:

GIS Maps Integration
Easy-to-use Rail Network Mapping Tool
Support for Rail Reference Data Imports
Network Traffic Simulations
Rail Capacity Modelling
Scheduling and optimisation tools
Rail headway calculations
Customisable driving strategy engine
Simulated Rail Breaks / Rail Maintenance and Outages
In addition to rail capacity and fleeting, GNOS utilises a physics-based rail simulation engine to provide insight into in-train forces and other significant physical constraints.

Key features include:

Standard and customisable Locomotive types, Wagons, and other pulled fleet options
Customisable simulation parameters
Longitudinal train dynamics
Applied tractive effort and braking curves
Multi-body dynamics simulation
Stopping distance calculations
Integration with track geometry, including superelevation
Energy and Fuel Modelling
Energy and Fuel Modelling are built into the physics and discrete event simulation, allowing for green-energy modelling and development of:

Fleet fuel and carbon emission analysis
Simulation of planned charging/Refueling Infrastructure, including location analysis
Overhead catenary and static charging options
Energy capture and regenerative braking
Capacity impact of required infrastructure
Viability analysis for potential net-zero technology options
Battery cycle and lifetime analysis
Industries & Applications
GNOS is tailored for industry professionals across sectors such as logistics, mining, and rail. Businesses use it to:

Simulate workflows and test different configurations.
Optimize operational efficiency while reducing costs.
Improve decision-making with reliable predictive modeling.
